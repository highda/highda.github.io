Small tools, carefully made.

Utilities for image processing, CSS, typography and music practice. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — files never leave your machine, and there is nothing to sign up for.

    Threshold-driven pixel sorting with fine control over direction, intervals and sort keys.

    Reduce images and video to the four-colour CGA palette. A fast, pure JavaScript pipeline built on Mediabunny.

    The original converter, running ImageMagick compiled to WebAssembly. Slower, with ImageMagick's dithering.
